AI training

Put AI to work in your business.

Practical, plain-English training that shows small-business owners and their teams how to use AI for the everyday work of running a business — from designing products to answering customers.

Ask about training What we cover

Who it's for

For owners who keep hearing about AI, but haven't had time to figure it out.

You don't need a technical background. We start with the jobs you already do every week and show you, step by step, how AI can take some of the load.

  • Owners and sole traders

    Learn the tools yourself and decide what's worth using in your business.

  • Small teams

    Get everyone using the same tools, safely and consistently.

  • Any local business

    Cafés, salons, trades, retail, clinics and professional services.

What we cover

Six areas where AI saves time from day one.

Products & services

Shape new offers, menus and packages, research what customers want, and test pricing ideas.

e.g. Plan a seasonal menu and name every dish.

Design & visuals

Create flyers, social graphics, signage and product images without a designer on call.

e.g. Turn a phone photo into a polished product shot.

Paperwork & admin

Draft quotes, invoices, policies, rosters and emails in minutes — then check them and send.

e.g. Write a clear refund policy in your own words.

Marketing

Plan a month of posts, write captions in your voice, and keep your Google Business Profile fresh.

e.g. Draft four weeks of Instagram posts in one sitting.

Customer service

Reply to reviews and enquiries faster, build an FAQ, and keep your tone friendly and consistent.

e.g. Draft a calm reply to a tricky review.

Using AI safely

Protect customer information, check facts before you publish, and know when not to rely on AI.

e.g. A simple checklist for your whole team.

How we teach

Learn by doing, on your own work.

Every session uses real tasks from your business. You'll leave with drafts you can actually use — not just a page of notes.

We show you how to ask AI for what you need, how to check and improve what it gives you, and how to make the result sound like you.

Formats

Training that fits around your business.

  • Group workshops

    Hands-on sessions with owners from other local businesses. Bring a laptop and your questions.

  • One-on-one sessions

    We sit down with you — at your business or online — and work through the tasks that matter most to you.

  • Team training

    Sessions for your staff, tailored to your tools, your customers and the way you work.

What you'll walk away with

Ready to use AI on Monday morning.

  • A shortlist of tools that suit your business and budget
  • Your own library of prompts for the tasks you do most
  • A checklist for using AI safely with customer information
  • A simple 30-day plan to build AI into your week
